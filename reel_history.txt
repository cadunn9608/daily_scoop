In the 1600s, the ultimate luxury ice cream flavor was seasoned with ambergris, a waxy substance coughed up by sperm whales. Aristocrats obsessed over this bizarre delicacy, describing the flavor as a mix of sweet vanilla and musk.
Victorian street vendors served ice cream in reusable glass cups called licking glasses, which were simply wiped with a dirty rag between customers. This bizarrely unhygienic practice turned the beloved frozen treat into a deadly super-spreader of tuberculosis.
During World War II, the US Navy spent one million dollars building a floating concrete ice cream parlor in the Pacific to boost troop morale. This bizarre military vessel did nothing but churn out fifteen hundred gallons of ice cream every single hour for battle-weary sailors.
